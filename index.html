Strategic trade access

Why produce in Mexico.

Mexico's trade agreements give your brand preferential access to North America, Europe, and Asia — from a single production base.

  • USMCA

    United States · Mexico · Canada. Tariff-free access to the largest consumer market in North America.

  • EU-Mexico Global Agreement

    Preferential trade access to the European Union.

  • CPTPP

    Trans-Pacific Partnership — preferential access to key Asia-Pacific markets.

Mexico City — central transport & logistics hubs, skilled food-production workforce, cost-effective manufacturing with global quality standards.
